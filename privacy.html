Privacy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What SignBuddy does with audio

When you press Start, SignBuddy captures the audio of the current tab (or your microphone, if you choose that) so it can turn speech into text and signs. SignBuddy itself does not record, save or upload this audio.

To turn speech into text, SignBuddy uses the speech recognition built into Google Chrome (the Web Speech API). Chrome may send that audio to Google's servers to recognise it. That processing is covered by Google's privacy policy. If you turn on On-device speech and your Chrome supports it, recognition happens on your computer instead.

What is stored

Only your settings (speed, size, captions on/off, character style) and where you placed the character on the screen. These are saved in Chrome's local extension storage on your computer. Captions and transcripts are not saved.

Optional local AI

If you turn on Better grammar, each sentence is sent to an Ollama model running on your own computer (localhost). It does not leave your computer.

What SignBuddy does not do

  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone.
  • It only reads audio while it is switched on, in the tab where you started it.
